01

Who operates this integration

Tony Leung is the operator and contact for this personal-use integration. Privacy, access, correction, or deletion questions can be sent to info@flow2.xyz. Do not include passwords, OAuth tokens, verification codes, or unnecessary private content in a support request.

This public website describes the integration. It does not provide public registration, collect Google passwords, or run the Google authorization flow.

02

Google data and permissions

Hermes accesses Google data only after the account owner authorizes it through Google OAuth. Depending on the permissions actually requested and granted, that can include:

  • Gmail: message metadata, message contents, attachments, drafts, and actions such as sending or modifying messages.
  • Calendar: calendars, availability, event details, attendees, and event creation or modification.
  • Drive, Docs, and Sheets: file metadata and contents, documents, spreadsheet cells, and requested file or document changes.
  • Authorization information: account identifiers and OAuth access or refresh tokens needed to call authorized Google APIs.

The actual permission boundary is the Google consent screen and the grants stored in the Google Account, not this list of possible functions. The operator is responsible for requesting only permissions needed for the enabled tasks.

03

Why data is used

Relevant Google data is used to carry out the owner’s tasks, such as answering a question about email, summarizing information, preparing a reply, scheduling an event, finding a file, or creating a document or spreadsheet output. Task instructions, resulting outputs, and operational diagnostics may also be processed to support those tasks and troubleshoot failures.

Google data is not sold, used for advertising or credit decisions, or shared with data brokers.

04

AI processing and sharing

Hermes is an AI-assisted agent, not a guarantee of local-only processing. When an AI-backed task uses Google content, relevant instructions and content may be sent from the operator-controlled Hermes runtime to the AI provider or intermediary configured for that task. This may include message text, event details, document contents, or task outputs.

The operator must review the active provider, routing, retention, and training settings before using Google data with an AI service. Only providers and configurations consistent with the user’s consent and Google’s Limited Use restrictions may be used. Google data must not be used to train or improve generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Google receives authorized API requests. Runtime hosting, AI, and other service providers may process the data necessary to provide the requested feature. Content sent as an email, calendar invitation, or shared file goes to the recipients selected for that task. Data may also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to investigate security abuse. There is no public sharing of Google account data by this informational website.

05

Storage, retention, and security

OAuth credentials are retained in the operator-controlled Hermes environment so the agent can refresh access without repeated sign-in. Relevant Google content and outputs may appear in the runtime’s task history, conversation logs, generated files, or operator-managed backups, depending on its configuration. This policy does not promise that every task is stateless or that all content is deleted immediately.

Tokens are retained while the connection is needed and until removed from the runtime; task records and outputs are retained while needed for the personal workflow or until the operator deletes them. No fixed automatic deletion period is represented here. Backups and provider-side records may have separate retention schedules.

Google API traffic uses HTTPS. Access to the runtime, credentials, logs, and backups must be restricted by the operator. No system can guarantee absolute security. Do not supply account passwords to Hermes or this website.

06

Revoke access and request deletion

You can remove the integration’s Google access at Google Account → Third-party connections. Select the Hermes connection and remove access. This stops future API access but does not itself delete copies already saved by the runtime, its providers, recipients, or backups.

For deletion of operator-controlled OAuth credentials, task records, logs, and saved outputs, contact info@flow2.xyz and identify the account or records concerned without sending secrets. The operator will verify the request and delete data under their control unless retention is required for legal or security reasons, explaining any applicable exception. Provider-side records follow the relevant provider’s deletion process and retention terms. You may also request access to or correction of operator-held personal information.

07

This website and support messages

The Hermes pages contain no advertising, analytics scripts, account forms, or application cookies. The website is hosted by Vercel. The hosting infrastructure may process standard request and security information, such as IP address, request time, requested page, and browser information, to deliver and protect the pages. This website does not receive your Google OAuth tokens or workspace content.

If you email the operator, your email address, message, and any attachments are used to handle the inquiry. External links and services have their own privacy policies.

08

Google Limited Use commitment

Hermes agent’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy and the Google Workspace API User Data and Developer Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google data is limited to the disclosed user-facing functions. Transfers are limited to providing those functions with consent, security needs, legal obligations, or other transfers expressly allowed by Google policy. Human access is limited to the account owner and policy-permitted situations such as explicit consent, security investigation, or legal requirements. These restrictions also apply to derived data and service providers.

09

Changes and audience

This integration is intended for the adult operator’s personal use, not for children or public distribution. Material changes to Google data handling require updated disclosures and any necessary renewed consent before the new use begins. The effective date above identifies this version.
