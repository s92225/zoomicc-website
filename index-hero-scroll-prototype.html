Case study 1 / Handbag consignment

Lume Lux handbag consignment

Lume Lux handles handbag consignment intake, client sign-off, receipts, inventory, Shopify records, and day-end spreadsheet work. The automation turns that intake flow into a cleaner operating system.

  • Clients complete most intake fields and sign off before the record moves forward.
  • Receipts, WhatsApp copies, inventory records, and sales data stay connected.
  • Shopify and CMS data feed Google Sheets for staff payout calculation.

Hover or focus a node to see how the Lume Lux automation works.

Case study 2 / Internal communication

Internal communication synchronisation system

A Hong Kong-headquartered MNC manages a large portfolio of malls and shopping arcades. The team needed a central way to prepare internal updates for PR, marketing, media handling, public enquiry response, and frontline incident routing.

  • Promotion and event data from mall apps is consolidated into distribution-ready material.
  • Frontline incident reports move to headquarters with department routing rules.
  • Microsoft Power Automate keeps the data stream and notification flow coordinated.
