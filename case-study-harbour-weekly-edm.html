Weekly EDM workflow

Harbour Weekly turned hundreds of news items into an AI-assisted newsletter draft

A publishing workflow that collects the week's Notion research, asks AI to shape it into a structured EDM, then stores the Google Docs draft back into the editorial record.

ClientHarbour Weekly
BusinessAI / automation editorial
WorkflowNotion to Google Docs
System roleWeekly draft engine

The pain point

The news was collected, but the weekly draft still took too much editorial time.

The team already kept AI and automation news in Notion. The bottleneck was turning more than a hundred raw items into a clear EDM with sections, links, analysis, and a ready-to-review draft.

01 / Volume

Too many stories

Hundreds of weekly items had to be reviewed before a usable issue could take shape.

02 / Structure

Changing themes

Each issue needed a different editorial hierarchy, not a fixed newsletter template.

03 / Handoff

Drafts lived apart

The final Google Docs draft and the Notion record needed to stay connected for review.

The Flow2 solution

Flow2 built a weekly drafting engine from research database to editable issue.

The automation filters the past seven days of Notion items, compresses the material into a single editorial brief, asks Gemini to write a structured Traditional Chinese issue, then creates a Google Docs draft and stores the link back in Notion.

Gather

Seven-day source window

Only the current week's research enters the drafting run.

Shape

AI acts as editor

The prompt builds sections, focus topics, data angles, and practical takeaways.

Record

Draft link returns to Notion

The issue is easy to review, approve, and find again later.

The outcome

The team moved from manual compilation to a repeatable editorial system.

The final asset is not an auto-sent email. It is a high-quality first draft with the right sections, links, and review trail, so editors spend time judging the issue instead of assembling it.
